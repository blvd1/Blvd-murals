How It Works

A clear process from first conversation to final protection.

01 — Consult

We talk through the wall, location, goals, timeline, budget, and overall direction for the project.

02 — Design

BLVD develops the concept and mockup, then refines the artwork with you before production begins.

03 — Install

The approved artwork is hand-painted on site with professional materials, equipment, and careful site coordination.

04 — Protect

When appropriate, the finished mural can be protected with UV-resistant and anti-graffiti coatings for added durability.

Built big.
Painted by hand.

BLVD Murals is led by artist Jay Morgan, co-founder of Austin art collective SprATX. With more than a decade of professional mural experience and roots in street art, the studio creates ambitious hand-painted work for major brands, businesses, developers, and communities.

Every project is approached as both artwork and environment—designed to feel specific to the wall, the client, and the people who experience it.
