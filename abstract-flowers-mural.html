Large-scale collaborative mural painting

BLVD contributed hands-on painting and production work to this large-scale collaborative mural. The project demonstrates the studio’s experience translating detailed artwork to a large wall while maintaining composition, finish, and visual impact at architectural scale.

BLVD works both independently and alongside creative teams, agencies, architects, developers, and production partners when a project calls for collaborative mural execution.
