Austin mural artist for custom, large-scale work

BLVD Murals is an Austin-based mural studio led by artist Jay Morgan, creating hand-painted artwork for businesses, brands, developers, hospitality spaces, events, public spaces, and community projects. Each mural is designed for its specific wall, setting, audience, and creative goal.

Custom mural design and painting

Projects can range from a single interior feature wall to large exterior murals and multi-surface installations. BLVD can develop original concepts and mockups, adapt approved creative for large-scale painting, coordinate site logistics, and carry the work through installation and final protection.

Murals for businesses, brands, and public spaces

BLVD works with restaurants, retailers, offices, hospitality groups, developers, creative agencies, architects, universities, municipalities, venues, festivals, and brands. Work can include custom murals, public art, hand-painted signage and lettering, branded environments, live painting, and experiential activations.

An experienced Austin mural studio

Jay Morgan brings more than a decade of professional mural experience and roots in Austin street art. BLVD approaches each commission as both artwork and environment, with attention to the architecture, viewing distance, materials, production conditions, and the people who will experience the finished wall.

From first idea to finished mural

The process begins with the location, wall dimensions, goals, timeline, budget, and creative direction. BLVD can then handle concept development, mockups, revisions, surface and access planning, hand painting, and protective coatings when appropriate.

Austin mural artist FAQs

Does BLVD design original murals?
Yes. BLVD can develop an original concept from the project brief, location, brand, and creative direction, then refine the design through mockups before painting begins.

Can BLVD paint both indoor and outdoor murals?
Yes. Projects can include interior and exterior walls, with materials and protective finishes selected for the surface and environment.

Does BLVD travel outside Austin?
Yes. BLVD is based in Austin and works on mural projects throughout Texas and nationwide.
