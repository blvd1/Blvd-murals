BLVD MURALS

The Bowie — Austin, Texas

Large-scale exterior mural / Architectural artwork

A landmark wall

At The Bowie in Austin, oversized white lettering stretches across a dark blue exterior wall, framed by a striking burst of yellow rays. Bright turquoise and red geometric fields complete the composition.

Designed for the setting

The artwork follows the building's expansive exterior wall, creating a dramatic focal point beside an elevated pool terrace and the surrounding city views. Its graphic forms and high-contrast palette make the mural legible at architectural scale.

Visual impact

By combining typography, color, and bold geometric composition, the mural turns a prominent exterior surface into a memorable part of the building's identity.
