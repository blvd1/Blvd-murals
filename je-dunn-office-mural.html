J.E. Dunn Office Mural

A bold, colorful office mural bringing expressive graphics and a city-inspired visual identity into a working environment.

The Project

This large-format interior wall artwork combines oversized JEDUNN lettering, an urban skyline silhouette, energetic shapes, and vivid color. The mural stretches across the office wall, creating a distinctive visual backdrop for the workspace.

Design & Visual Impact

Bright reds, yellows, blues, greens, and purples contrast with dark architectural forms. Layered graffiti-inspired marks and graphic elements give the composition movement, while the skyline and bold typography anchor the design in its environment.

The Result

The finished wall transforms a functional office interior into a memorable branded space, demonstrating how custom murals can bring personality and visual impact to commercial environments.
