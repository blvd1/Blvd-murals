Commercial murals built for Austin spaces

BLVD Murals designs and hand-paints custom commercial murals for Austin restaurants, retail spaces, offices, hospitality projects, mixed-use developments, venues, campuses, and public-facing environments. Each project is developed around the architecture, brand, audience, and way people experience the space.

Commercial mural services

Projects can include interior and exterior murals, branded environments, large-scale wall art, hand-painted signage and lettering, public art, experiential brand activations, live painting, and artwork for new construction or existing spaces. BLVD can support projects from early creative direction and mockups through site planning, production, installation, and protective coatings.

For brands, developers, architects, agencies, and businesses

BLVD works directly with business owners and brands as well as creative agencies, architects, general contractors, developers, hospitality groups, universities, municipalities, and event teams. The goal is artwork that feels specific to the wall and the people who will see it—not a generic graphic enlarged to fit a surface.

From concept to final coat

The process starts with the wall, project goals, timeline, budget, and creative direction. From there, BLVD develops the concept and mockup, plans materials and access, coordinates production, and hand-paints the approved artwork on site. UV-resistant and anti-graffiti coatings can be added when appropriate for the location and surface.

Austin-based, available across Texas and nationwide

BLVD Murals is based in Austin, Texas and takes on commercial mural projects throughout Central Texas, across the state, and nationwide. For clients managing projects in multiple markets, BLVD can coordinate mural production while maintaining a consistent creative direction and finish.

Commercial mural FAQs

What types of Austin mural projects does BLVD take on?
Commercial interiors and exteriors, branded murals, public art, hospitality and retail artwork, office murals, developments, venues, events, activations, signage, and other large-scale painted installations.

Can BLVD create the mural design?
Yes. Projects can include concept development, creative direction, mockups, revisions, site planning, painting, and final protection.

Does BLVD work with architects, agencies, and contractors?
Yes. BLVD can work directly with a client or collaborate with architects, designers, agencies, developers, general contractors, and production partners as part of a larger project team.

Does BLVD only work in Austin?
No. Austin is home base, with projects available throughout Texas and across the United States.
